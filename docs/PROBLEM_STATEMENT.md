# GreenPulse: Problem Statement

**Project:** GreenPulse — Community Climate Action and Environmental Monitoring App

**UN Sustainable Development Goal:** SDG 13 — Climate Action

## 1. Background of the Problem

Climate change is an increasingly serious global issue that contributes to extreme weather events, flooding, intense heat, and other environmental risks. These challenges can affect the safety, health, and daily activities of local communities. However, residents may have difficulty accessing information about local environmental concerns, reporting problems to the appropriate authorities, and participating in organized climate action activities.

Environmental concerns such as improper waste disposal, flooding risks, excessive heat, and other community hazards may go unreported or remain unresolved when residents lack a convenient way to communicate their observations and monitor the progress of their reports. Information about community cleanups, environmental campaigns, and climate-related advisories may also be scattered across different communication channels.

## 2. Problem Statement

Residents need an accessible and organized platform where they can report local environmental concerns, access relevant environmental information, track the status of their submitted reports, and participate in community climate action activities. Without a centralized platform, communication between residents and community administrators may be less organized, making it harder to monitor reported concerns and encourage continued public participation in environmental protection.

GreenPulse aims to address this problem by providing a mobile application that connects community residents with environmental reporting, monitoring information, report-status updates, climate advisories, and community activities in one place.

## 3. Target Users

- **Community Residents:** People who want to report environmental concerns, receive relevant advisories, and participate in local environmental initiatives.
- **Community Administrators:** Authorized personnel who review submitted reports, update their status, and share environmental announcements and activities.

## 4. Proposed Solution

GreenPulse is a community-focused mobile application designed to support local climate awareness and action. Its proposed features include:

1. **Environmental Issue Reporting** — Allows residents to submit reports about environmental concerns, including descriptions, photos, and location information where available.
2. **Report Status Tracking** — Enables users to check whether reports are submitted, under review, in progress, or resolved.
3. **Environmental Monitoring** — Presents available local environmental information and advisories to help users stay informed.
4. **Climate Advisories and Announcements** — Shares relevant environmental notices and community updates.
5. **Community Activities** — Provides information about cleanups, tree-planting activities, and other community environmental initiatives.
6. **Administrator Report Management** — Allows authorized administrators to review submitted reports and update their progress.

## 5. Project Goal

The goal of GreenPulse is to encourage community participation in climate action by making environmental reporting, access to relevant information, and participation in local environmental activities more convenient and organized.

## 6. Alignment with UN SDG 13: Climate Action

GreenPulse supports SDG 13 by promoting climate awareness, encouraging community involvement, and helping residents and community administrators organize responses to local environmental concerns. The application aims to complement existing community response efforts by providing a convenient platform for environmental reporting, information sharing, and climate action participation. It does not replace official emergency services, environmental monitoring agencies, or government reporting systems.

## 7. Scope and Limitations

GreenPulse focuses on environmental issue reporting, report-status tracking, available environmental information, climate advisories, and community environmental activities. Its features depend on information and updates provided by authorized administrators and available data sources. Environmental monitoring information may not be available in real time, and the accuracy of reports depends on the information submitted by users and verified by authorized personnel. The application does not replace official emergency services or guarantee that every reported concern will be resolved.

## 8. Expected Impact

GreenPulse aims to improve community awareness, make environmental concern reporting more organized, increase transparency in report tracking, and encourage participation in local climate action activities. By bringing environmental reporting, information, and community initiatives together in one platform, the application hopes to strengthen communication between residents and community administrators and promote shared responsibility for environmental protection.

## 9. Conclusion

GreenPulse is designed to support community-level climate action by providing residents with an accessible platform to report environmental concerns, access relevant information, monitor report progress, and discover environmental activities. Through its proposed features and alignment with UN SDG 13, the project aims to encourage greater public participation, improve community communication, and contribute to more organized local environmental initiatives.
