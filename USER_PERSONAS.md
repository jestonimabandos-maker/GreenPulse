# GreenPulse: A Community Climate Action and Environmental Monitoring Application

## User Personas

**SDG Alignment:** SDG 13 — Climate Action

These are preliminary fictional personas created to guide the design and development of GreenPulse. They represent potential users who may benefit from environmental information, climate awareness resources, environmental concern reporting, and community-based climate action. The personas should be validated and updated through user interviews or surveys.

## 1. Climate-Conscious Student

**Name:** Jhonroi Umbokan  
**Age:** 24  
**Occupation:** College Student  
**Role:** Primary User

### About

A college student who wants to understand climate change and learn how everyday actions can help reduce environmental impact. He uses mobile applications to access information and discover activities relevant to his community.

### Goals

- Learn about climate change and its effects on the community.
- Access environmental information through a mobile application.
- Discover practical ways to adopt sustainable habits.
- Participate in climate awareness campaigns.

### Frustrations

- Difficulty finding reliable and understandable climate information.
- Limited awareness of local environmental activities.
- Uncertainty about which daily practices can help address climate change.

### Needs

- Easy-to-understand climate education resources.
- Accessible environmental updates and information.
- Practical sustainability tips.
- Information about community climate action activities.

### How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse aims to provide accessible climate education, environmental information, and activity announcements to help students become more informed and involved in climate action.

### Design Implications

The GreenPulse interface should provide a simple and accessible experience for students who want to learn about climate change and environmental conditions.

- Climate awareness cards with short, easy-to-understand information.
- Environmental monitoring cards for available environmental readings.
- Navigation buttons that provide easy access to climate education and sustainability tips.
- Readable typography, clear icons, and consistent green styling.
- A simple Home Dashboard that highlights important environmental information.

## 2. Community Environmental Advocate

**Name:** Gebb Ebero  
**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Community Volunteer  
**Role:** Secondary User

### About

A community volunteer who supports environmental protection and encourages residents to participate in activities such as tree planting, waste reduction campaigns, and neighborhood environmental awareness programs.

### Goals

- Discover community-based climate action activities.
- Encourage residents to participate in environmental programs.
- Share environmental awareness with other community members.
- Support initiatives that improve local environmental conditions.

### Frustrations

- Difficulty discovering upcoming environmental activities.
- Limited access to organized information about climate programs.
- Challenges encouraging consistent community participation.

### Needs

- A centralized page for climate action activities.
- Clear activity schedules and participation instructions.
- Accessible educational and awareness materials.
- Convenient ways to discover and share environmental initiatives, if supported.

### How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse aims to make climate action opportunities easier to discover and help environmental advocates encourage greater community participation in sustainable activities.

### Design Implications

The GreenPulse interface should make community climate action activities easy to discover and understand.

- Climate action activity cards displaying the activity name, date, and location.
- An activity details screen containing descriptions and participation instructions.
- Clear buttons for registration or indicating interest, if implemented.
- Organized activity categories for easier browsing.
- Consistent icons and visual styles across activity-related screens.

## 3. Local Environmental Monitoring Coordinator

**Name:** Maria Cristina Villanueva  
**Age:** 42  
**Occupation:** Community Development or Environmental Program Staff Member  
**Role:** Stakeholder Persona

### About

A potential community or local government staff member involved in environmental awareness, climate resilience programs, or monitoring environmental concerns. She needs organized information to support community planning and environmental initiatives.

### Goals

- Access available environmental information relevant to the community.
- Identify recurring environmental concerns reported by residents.
- Promote climate awareness and preparedness activities.
- Support community programs that address climate-related challenges.

### Frustrations

- Environmental information may be scattered across different sources.
- Difficulty identifying patterns in community-reported concerns.
- Limited awareness of residents' participation in environmental programs.
- Challenges communicating environmental updates to the community.

### Needs

- An organized environmental information dashboard.
- Accessible community environmental reports, where implemented.
- Tools for sharing climate-related announcements.
- Clear information about community activities and environmental concerns.

### How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse could provide a centralized platform for accessing environmental information, reviewing community concerns, and promoting climate-related programs. Administrative monitoring and report management would depend on the features implemented and the user's authorized access.

### Design Implications

The GreenPulse interface should organize environmental information and reported concerns in a clear and structured format.

- Environmental summary cards displaying available readings or updates.
- Report lists and status badges to distinguish submitted, under-review, in-progress, and resolved concerns, where supported.
- A report details screen for reviewing the information provided by residents.
- Clear information hierarchy and consistent status colors.
- Administrative controls only if authorized staff access is included in the project scope.

## 5. Week 2 Design System and Prototype Priorities

Based on the identified user needs, the following priorities will guide the GreenPulse Component Design System and Clickable Interactive Figma Prototype.

### 5.1 Visual Consistency

- Maintain GreenPulse's existing green visual identity.
- Use consistent typography, font sizes, spacing, and corner radii.
- Apply consistent icon styles and button designs across screens.
- Establish reusable color styles for primary actions, secondary actions, backgrounds, and report statuses.

### 5.2 Reusable UI Components

- **Environmental Monitoring Cards:** Present available environmental indicators and related information.
- **Climate Awareness Cards:** Display educational content and sustainability tips.
- **Climate Action Activity Cards:** Show activity descriptions, dates, and locations.
- **Report Status Badges:** Indicate the current status of environmental reports.
- **Navigation Buttons:** Allow users to move between the Home Dashboard and main features.
- **Report Form Components:** Provide input fields, category selection, location details, and photo attachment options, where supported.

### 5.3 Clickable Interactive Prototype

The Figma prototype should connect the main screens and demonstrate the intended user experience.

- Home Dashboard to Environmental Monitoring.
- Home Dashboard to Environmental Concern Reporting.
- Home Dashboard to Climate Action Activities.
- Home Dashboard to Climate Awareness.
- Environmental Concern Reporting to report confirmation and My Reports, if included.
- Feature screens back to the Home Dashboard through clear navigation controls.

The prototype should demonstrate the interactions that are included in the actual design. Features that are not yet implemented should remain proposed rather than being presented as completed functionality.

## 6. Research and Validation

These personas are preliminary and represent possible GreenPulse user groups, including students, environmental volunteers, and community environmental coordinators. They should be validated through surveys or interviews with potential users to understand their actual experiences, environmental information needs, technology preferences, and barriers to participating in climate action.

Feedback from potential users should guide the refinement of these personas and help the team prioritize features that are practical, accessible, and relevant to SDG 13 — Climate Action.

The Week 2 design system and clickable prototype will use these preliminary findings as a starting point. Usability feedback and prototype testing should be used to identify navigation difficulties, improve component consistency, and refine the overall user experience.

## 7. Expected Outcome

The GreenPulse user personas provide a foundation for understanding potential users and translating their goals, frustrations, and needs into design decisions.

By connecting the personas to specific UI components and prototype interactions, the team can create a more consistent, accessible, and user-centered application design. These design decisions aim to support environmental awareness, access to environmental information, reporting of local environmental concerns, and participation in community climate action.

All personas and proposed features should be reviewed and refined as the project progresses, ensuring that the final application remains aligned with its objectives and UN Sustainable Development Goal 13 — Climate Action.
