GreenPulse: A Community Climate Action and Environmental Monitoring Application

 User Personas

SDG Alignment:* SDG 13 — Climate Action

These are preliminary fictional personas created to guide the design of GreenPulse. They represent potential users who may benefit from environmental information, climate awareness resources, and community-based climate action. The personas should be validated and updated through user interviews or surveys.

1. Climate-Conscious Student

 Name: Jhonroi Umbokan
 Age: 24
 Occupation: College Student
 Role: Primary User

 About

A college student who wants to understand climate change and learn how everyday actions can help reduce environmental impact. He uses mobile applications to access information and discover activities relevant to his community.

Goals

- Learn about climate change and its effects on the community.
- Access environmental information through a mobile application.
- Discover practical ways to adopt sustainable habits.
- Participate in climate awareness campaigns.

Frustrations

- Difficulty finding reliable and understandable climate information.
- Limited awareness of local environmental activities.
- Uncertainty about which daily practices can help address climate change.

Needs

- Easy-to-understand climate education resources.
- Accessible environmental updates and information.
- Practical sustainability tips.
- Information about community climate action activities.

 How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse aims to provide accessible climate education, environmental information, and activity announcements to help students become more informed and involved in climate action.

2. Community Environmental Advocate

Name: Gebb Ebero
Age: 28
Occupation: Community Volunteer
Role: Secondary User

About

A community volunteer who supports environmental protection and encourages residents to participate in activities such as tree planting, waste reduction campaigns, and neighborhood environmental awareness programs.

Goals

- Discover community-based climate action activities.
- Encourage residents to participate in environmental programs.
- Share environmental awareness with other community members.
- Support initiatives that improve local environmental conditions.

Frustrations

- Difficulty discovering upcoming environmental activities.
- Limited access to organized information about climate programs.
- Challenges encouraging consistent community participation.

Needs

- A centralized page for climate action activities.
- Clear activity schedules and participation instructions.
- Accessible educational and awareness materials.
- Convenient ways to discover and share environmental initiatives, if supported.

How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse aims to make climate action opportunities easier to discover and help environmental advocates encourage greater community participation in sustainable activities.

3. Local Environmental Monitoring Coordinator

Name: Maria Cristina Villanueva
Age: 42
Occupation: Community Development or Environmental Program Staff Member
Role: Stakeholder Persona

About

A potential community or local government staff member involved in environmental awareness, climate resilience programs, or monitoring environmental concerns. She needs organized information to support community planning and environmental initiatives.

Goals

- Access available environmental information relevant to the community.
- Identify recurring environmental concerns reported by residents.
- Promote climate awareness and preparedness activities.
- Support community programs that address climate-related challenges.

Frustrations

- Environmental information may be scattered across different sources.
- Difficulty identifying patterns in community-reported concerns.
- Limited awareness of residents' participation in environmental programs.
- Challenges communicating environmental updates to the community.

Needs

- An organized environmental information dashboard.
- Accessible community environmental reports, where implemented.
- Tools for sharing climate-related announcements.
- Clear information about community activities and environmental concerns.

How GreenPulse Helps

GreenPulse could provide a centralized platform for accessing environmental information, reviewing community concerns, and promoting climate-related programs. Administrative monitoring and report management would depend on the features implemented and the user's authorized access.

4. Research and Validation

These personas are preliminary and represent possible GreenPulse user groups, including students, environmental volunteers, and community environmental coordinators. They should be validated through surveys or interviews with potential users to understand their actual experiences, environmental information needs, technology preferences, and barriers to participating in climate action.

Feedback from potential users should guide the refinement of these personas and help the team prioritize features that are practical, accessible, and relevant to SDG 13 — Climate Action.
